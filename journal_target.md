## Fit

- JSS publishes work across software engineering and requires evidence to
  support claims through empirical studies, simulation, formal proofs, or other
  validation.
- OrderDeltaBench is an empirical software-engineering study of interface
  design, structured output reliability, and deterministic verification for
  large-language-model transaction agents.
- JSS reports a 15% acceptance rate, so this should be treated as a serious
  journal submission target rather than a guaranteed fit.
